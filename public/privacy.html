This app is a private tool that [Your Company] uses to run email outreach from its own Google accounts. This page explains what the app does with those accounts.

Google account data

When a team member connects a Google account, the app receives:

  • The account's email address.
  • Access to its Gmail, which the app uses to send email from that account and to read incoming mail so it can detect replies, opt-outs and bounces.
  • A login token, stored encrypted so the app can keep working after sign-in.

How it is used

This data is used only to send outreach from the connected account, record replies, stop follow-ups when someone replies or opts out, and show results in the team's dashboard. The app stores details of replies to its outreach, such as sender, subject and text. The dashboard's All mail view shows the rest of the inbox to signed-in team members, read live from Gmail and not stored.

Sharing

We do not sell this data or use it for advertising. It is shared only with the service providers that run the app, which host its code and database, with Anthropic, whose Claude AI reads incoming replies so the app can sort them, or if the law requires it. Google data is not used to train AI models.

This app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can disconnect an account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To have its stored data deleted, email us at the address below.
